2 Information We Collect & How It Is Used

Tic Tac Toe - Fun Master is built as a privacy-friendly casual game that requires no user registration, account sign-in, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords to play.

The categories of data processed include:

  • Local Device Storage (On-Device Only): Your chosen in-game player name, selected avatar icon, virtual coin balances, unlocked marks (X and O), audio sound settings, and match statistics are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage. We never upload or synchronize this private game state to our own external servers.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable technical details such as your device model, Android operating system version, screen resolution, locale language, and network connection status collected through standard Android OS runtime calls.
  • Crash & Performance Logs: Anonymized diagnostic crash reports to identify and rectify runtime bugs, ensuring smooth and responsive 60 FPS gameplay.

3 Advertising Partners & Google AdMob Disclosures

To maintain Tic Tac Toe - Fun Master as a 100% free-to-play game for players worldwide, we integrate third-party advertising SDKs from Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): Used by AdMob for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, ad measurement, and personalized/non-personalized ad delivery based on your device consent settings.
  • Approximate Geolocation: Derived from IP address at country/city level to serve region-relevant ads.
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information on ad views and completion of voluntary rewarded video ads (used for earning +500 free bonus coins, unlocking custom avatars, or picking X/O marks).
Rewarded Ads Transparency: Watching rewarded video ads in the game is always voluntary and initiated by the player. You are never forced to watch rewarded ads to play standard matches or local Pass & Play games.

Review Google’s comprehensive privacy practices: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
